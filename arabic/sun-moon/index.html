Sun & Moon Letters

الحُرُوف الشَّمْسِيَّة وَالقَمَرِيَّة

When ال (al-) meets a sun letter, the laam goes silent and the letter doubles: الشَّمْس is “ash-shams”, not “al-shams”. Moon letters keep the laam: القَمَر stays “al-qamar”.

☀️ Sun letters — the laam melts

Say it doubled: التَّمْر → at-tamr · الشَّمْس → ash-shams · النُّور → an-nur

🌙 Moon letters — the laam stays

Say the laam clearly: القَمَر → al-qamar · الكِتَاب → al-kitab · البَيْت → al-bayt
